# The Line — what rises, what stays

A client knowledge cartridge is a **sealed container.** This file defines the one rule that makes the whole model honest, and that a data-sensitive client (or our own legal) will ask about directly.

## ↑ Rises into `cosi-knowledge` (the shared brain)

Only **de-identified, generalized patterns and craft.**

> e.g. *"safety-sensitive brands respond better to a two-column safety report"*

## ↓ Stays in this cartridge (never leaves un-sanitized)

**Raw assets, data, and anything identifying.**

> e.g. *"Aurora's brand blue is Pantone 2935c"* — stays in Aurora's cartridge.

## Why it matters

The promise to the client is: *"Your knowledge lives in your container — never in a shared box with anyone else's. The only thing in our shared brain is general craft we've earned, never your raw, private data."* If we ever synthesize a client's specifics into the shared brain, that promise is quietly broken.

This is the **same boundary** as the CoSi [data-handling standard](https://github.com/Collier-Simon/cosi-standards/blob/main/standards/data-handling.md). The cartridge wall and "what's allowed to leave a client's container to a non-local model" are one line — which is exactly why it holds up under scrutiny.

## Sign-off (gate)

A **filled** cartridge (real client data) requires legal/contract sign-off on this boundary first — specifically: *what, precisely, is allowed to generalize from this client to help others, and in what form.* Until `cartridge.yaml → boundary.legal_signoff: true`, this stays a structural template with no real data.
