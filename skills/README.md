# skills/ — subject-specific skills

The skills and specialists built **for this subject** live here. They compose your general-purpose skills with this bundle's brand + knowledge to produce work specific to this subject.

**The test for what belongs here (dotKnowledge SPEC.md §3):** does this capability make sense independent of which subject's bundle is mounted, or does it only exist because of something particular to *this* subject? Generic operating mechanism (a daily-briefing routine, a session-wrap-up ritual — anything that works the same way regardless of whose bundle is mounted) belongs in the harness, and never here. A capability that only exists *because of* this subject's own facts — a brand-voice checker built from their actual voice rules, a format-specific generator tied to their real deliverables — belongs here, because it's part of what makes this bundle a complete, portable artifact.

> Template — empty. Real subject-specific skills are added when the bundle is stamped. Keep them subject-specific; anything reusable across subjects belongs in your general skills, not here.

Pattern: `Shared skill + this bundle's brand + knowledge = subject-specific work.`
